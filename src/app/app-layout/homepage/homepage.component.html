All African Games 2023

The 13th edition of the All African Games marked a
historic moment for Ghana as the nation hosted
Africa’s best handball nations in a week-long festival
of sport and unity. The competition brought together
10 countries across the continent, competing fiercely
for glory on the court. The Ghanaian national team,
driven by home support and months of preparation,
delivered a spirited performance, narrowly missing
out on the semifinals but winning the hearts of fans.

IHF Trophy Tournament 2025

Ghana’s U-20 and U-18 national handball teams made
a strong statement at the International Handball
Federation (IHF) Trophy Africa Zone III tournament
held in Abidjan. The tournament featured seven West
African nations including Nigeria, Benin, Togo, Liberia,
and host nation Côte d’Ivoire.

Ghana’s youth teams demonstrated impressive
technical and tactical development, with the U-20 side
earning a 3rd place finish after a tightly contested
bronze medal match against Benin.
