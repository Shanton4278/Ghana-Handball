Our History
Handball was officially introduced in Ghana on 24th March 1975 by the late Mr. Gabriel Missodey, then Volta Regional Sports Development Officer. He was assisted by his brother, Emile Missodey, a senior officer with the Ghana National Fire Service.
In April 1976, Mr. Cate Caesar, a visiting lecturer at the Specialist Training College Physical Education Department (now the University of Education, Winneba), organized the first-ever handball clinic at the Osu end of the Accra Sports Stadium. This week-long event introduced the basic skills of the sport and was supported by Mr. S. K. Hlordze and the Missodey brothers — all recognized handball trainers under the National Sports Council.

Later in 1976, the Missodey brothers founded Great Fingers International Club, inspired by Alex Asiedu, then Chief Sports Organiser of the National Sports Council, and Mr. S. K. Hlordze, the Deputy Chief Sports Organiser.
On 11th November 1976, the Handball Association of Ghana (HAG) was officially inaugurated at the Ministry of Sports by Lt. Col. Simpe Asante, Special Assistant to the Commissioner of Sports. Mr. L. T. Cate Caesar of the Physical Education Department of the Ministry of Education was appointed as the first President of HAG.
The first official handball match in Ghana was played on 16th December 1976 at the Accra Sports Stadium. The game featured Great Fingers International Handball Club and Specialist Training College (now UEW Winneba). Great Fingers emerged victorious, winning by 10 goals to 9. That same year, Ghana University Sports Association (GUSA) introduced handball into their competitions. However, due to limited infrastructure and logistics, growth was slow until recent years.
In early 1977, a one-week selection exercise ("justify-your-inclusion") was held at the Accra Sports Stadium. Players were selected from STC P.E. students, Great Fingers International, and other emerging talents to form the first national handball teams (men and women).
On 25th April 1977, the selected teams began residential camping at the Specialist Training College in Winneba under the leadership of Togbe Asiamah II (Jack Agbo), then with the District Education Office in Sunyani.
Ghana played its first international match on 28th May 1977 against Togo during the ECOWAS Games in Accra. Despite valiant efforts, Ghana lost both matches: Men: Ghana 5 – 32 Togo Women: Ghana 6 – 15 Togo From humble beginnings and early challenges, handball in Ghana has grown to become a recognized and respected sport, nurtured by passionate pioneers and dedicated athletes. The Handball Association of Ghana remains committed to promoting the sport and developing talent across all regions.

Our Mission

“Our mission is to develop and promote handball in Ghana by
creating inclusive programs, nurturing talent at all levels, and
providing pathways for athletes, coaches, and officials to excel.
Through innovation, strategic collaborations, and community engagement, we aim to build a strong foundation for the sport,
foster national pride, and position Ghana as a leader in handball
across Africa and beyond.”

Our Vision

“To establish handball as Ghana’s most celebrated and
accessible sport by fostering grassroots participation,
developing world-class talent, forging strategic partnerships,
and creating sustainable opportunities that unite communities
and inspire future generations.”
